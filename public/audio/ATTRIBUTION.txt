French speech clips in this folder were generated with the Piper fr_FR-siwis-medium voice.

Voice model: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/fr/fr_FR/siwis/medium
Model card and dataset license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
Dataset: SIWIS, https://datashare.is.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/2353
Piper project: https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl

The included MP3 files are generated speech clips for the Pas à pas French-learning app.
